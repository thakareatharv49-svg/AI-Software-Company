# M34 — Company Dashboard

## Goal

Provide one read-only view of the autonomous company's operational state.

## Implementation

`CompanyDashboard` aggregates company, project, agent, task, runtime, memory, QA, security, cost, deployment, failure, research, metric, and audit providers without owning their persistence.

## Company Integration

`CompanyOperations.dashboard()` connects the dashboard to actual execution history and research reports produced by the company operating cycle.

The dashboard therefore reports real company state rather than placeholder counters. Unsupported subsystems remain explicit empty providers until their milestones implement them.
