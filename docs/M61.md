# M61 — Resilient Factory Restore

## Goal

Keep one malformed or temporarily unavailable durable mission-job record from aborting recovery of unrelated pending projects.

## Behavior

- Each pending snapshot is reconciled against its durable mission-job lifecycle before it is re-queued.
- If reading or sanitizing that mission's durable job raises, the factory logs a generic diagnostic and skips that snapshot.
- The factory does not fall back to an older project snapshot when the durable lifecycle cannot be verified, avoiding accidental resurrection of completed or cancelled work.
- Later independent snapshots continue restoring normally.
- Error logs do not include exception text or mission content that could contain sensitive values.

## Validation

A regression test supplies two pending snapshots and a job store that raises for the first mission. It verifies the first snapshot is skipped and the second mission is restored.

## Scope boundary

This protects startup recovery from per-record durable job read failures. It does not claim that the underlying database or job store is healthy; the affected mission remains un-restored until the durable lifecycle can be checked again.
