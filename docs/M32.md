# M32 — Real-World Research Engine

## Goal

Provide source-backed research that can feed real company work.

## Implementation

- `ResearchSource`, `ResearchFinding`, and `ResearchReport` preserve evidence and source identity.
- `StaticResearchProvider` provides deterministic research for tests and controlled environments.
- `UrlResearchProvider` can fetch supplied real-world URLs through a bounded HTTP request.
- Duplicate source identities are rejected before findings are produced.

## Company Integration

`CompanyOperations.research()` uses the research engine as the first stage of the company operating cycle. Research findings are then passed into M33 handoffs rather than remaining isolated test data.

## Safety

The provider accepts explicit URLs rather than silently crawling arbitrary destinations. Research remains source-backed and inspectable.
