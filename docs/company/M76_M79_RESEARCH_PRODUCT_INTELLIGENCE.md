# M76-M79 Research & Product Intelligence

## M76 — Advanced Web Research
AdvancedResearchEngine decomposes a research objective into traceable subqueries and delegates source retrieval to the existing evidence-backed ResearchEngine.

## M77 — Market & Competitive Intelligence
CompetitiveIntelligence normalizes competitor strengths, weaknesses, and evidence into comparable market signals and identifies gaps.

## M78 — Autonomous Product Discovery
ProductDiscovery transforms source-backed research reports into ranked product candidates for a target user.

## M79 — Autonomous Roadmap Generation
RoadmapGenerator converts prioritized product candidates into phased execution items.

All retrieval is provider-injected: the core remains deterministic and testable, while real web/search providers can be connected without changing the research and product models.
